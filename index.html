DPL 2026 – Payment, Cancellation & Refund Policy

  1. The team registration fee is ₹3,100 per team.
  2. Payment must be made only after the team has been officially selected by the DPL administration.
  3. Once payment has been successfully completed, the registration fee is non-refundable.
  4. Teams cannot claim a refund simply because they decide not to participate, withdraw from the tournament or are unable to attend.
  5. Captains must carefully verify all payment details before making payment.
  6. If a payment is accidentally duplicated or received in excess, the organiser will review the transaction and address the issue appropriately.
  7. If the tournament is cancelled by the organiser, postponed indefinitely or cannot be conducted, the organiser will communicate the applicable refund or alternative arrangements to the participating teams.

By submitting payment, the team captain confirms that they have read and accepted these terms.
